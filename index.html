Game Hall

Pick a game. Play the computer, pass one device around, or send a link so family can play from their own phone or tablet. No accounts needed.

Install Game Hall

Give it its own icon and window. Computer and Pass & play games then work even without internet.

Chrome or Edge (Windows, Mac, Chromebook)

Click the install icon at the right end of the address bar, or open the ⋮ menu and choose Cast, save and share › Install page as app (Edge: Apps › Install this site as an app).

Safari on a Mac

Choose File › Add to Dock.

iPad or iPhone

Tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.

Android

Open the ⋮ menu and tap Add to Home screen or Install app.

Game Hall runs in your browser. Online games connect your devices directly; the person who creates a game keeps it open while you play.
